Tender Intelligence questions

Does MedicHall replace the official tender documents?

No. Extracted facts and answers help users navigate the documents, but the source documents and official notice remain authoritative.

Can MedicHall analyze uploaded tender documents?

Yes. Authorized users can import supported tender documents and URLs into their private workspace, subject to platform limits and source availability.

Are company match scores public?

No. Company-specific tender matches, evidence and conversations are private operational data.
