Questions about distributor discovery

Can manufacturers find distributors on MedicHall?

Yes. Manufacturers can browse public company profiles and create a structured matchmaking profile to evaluate relevant partner candidates.

Does every company in the directory represent a distributor?

No. The directory contains multiple company roles, including manufacturers, distributors, suppliers and buyers. Each role is shown as supplied by the company.

Can companies contact a match directly?

Companies can request a business connection. Accepted connections can continue through messaging and meeting workflows inside MedicHall.
