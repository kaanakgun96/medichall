Matchmaking questions

How does medical device matchmaking work?

Companies create structured profiles describing what they offer and seek. MedicHall compares explicit evidence such as products, markets, certifications and commercial preferences, then presents reviewable fit explanations.

Does a match automatically contact another company?

No. A company chooses whether to send a business connection request. No automatic outreach is sent merely because a match exists.

Can connected companies schedule a video meeting?

Yes. Accepted connections can propose multiple times, confirm a meeting and use secure video access issued to authenticated participants.
