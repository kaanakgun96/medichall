For distributors

Commercial and regulatory verification still matters

Company-supplied certifications, specifications, images and documents must be verified directly before purchase, distribution or regulatory decisions.

Marketplace questions

Can buyers send RFQs on MedicHall?

Yes. Buyers can select a product, enter structured request details, review the intended recipients and submit a quotation request.

Does MedicHall verify every product specification?

No. Public specifications and documents are supplied by the listing company and should be verified during commercial and regulatory due diligence.

Is MedicHall only for manufacturers?

No. The marketplace and directory support manufacturers, distributors, suppliers, buyers and other medical-industry company roles.
