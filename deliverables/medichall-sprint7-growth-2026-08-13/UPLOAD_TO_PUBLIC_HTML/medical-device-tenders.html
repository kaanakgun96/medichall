What you can evaluate before signing in

Public metadata is deliberately useful but bounded. It helps a manufacturer, distributor or supplier decide whether to investigate, while private commercial reasoning remains private.

Opportunity basics

Notice title, contracting buyer, country and relevant deadline where present in the source data.

Procurement classification

CPV codes and notice categories provide a first structured route into medical-device relevance.

Source attribution

MedicHall links to official procurement notices; it does not present itself as the contracting authority.

From notice to evidence

When a notice looks relevant, an authenticated company can move into AI Tender Intelligence. Document Intelligence extracts facts with evidence references; users still verify the source documents and official notice.

Decision boundary

MedicHall supports discovery and analysis. It does not submit bids, replace legal or regulatory review, or make an official procurement decision for a company.

Questions about medical tenders

Where does MedicHall tender information come from?

MedicHall presents public procurement metadata and links users to the applicable official notice. The official source remains authoritative.

Is tender analysis public?

No. Company-specific matches, imported documents, evidence and Ask MedicHall conversations remain inside the authenticated Partner Portal.

Can a company monitor tenders for its products?

Yes. A completed company and product profile can be used for private matching and saved-search workflows in the Partner Portal.
