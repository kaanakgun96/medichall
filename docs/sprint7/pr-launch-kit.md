## F. 250-word description

MedicHall is an Open Beta medical B2B platform designed to connect the work that often happens across separate company directories, procurement feeds, product catalogs, tender documents and communication tools.

Public visitors can discover products from approved company profiles, explore manufacturers, distributors, suppliers, buyers and other medical-industry businesses, and browse factual European medical tender metadata linked to official procurement notices. The company directory preserves the role each business provides rather than presenting every company as a manufacturer.

Inside an authenticated workspace, companies can build structured product and matchmaking profiles. MedicHall’s Tender Intelligence workflow can process supported tender documents and official HTTPS sources, extract reviewable facts, preserve evidence and support grounded questions about the selected tender. Product taxonomy and lot-level context help resolve medical product terminology while unknown information remains visible as a gap. Users remain responsible for the official notice, source documents, eligibility, regulatory requirements and every bid decision.

Partner matchmaking compares explicit evidence such as products, markets, certifications and commercial preferences. A match does not automatically contact another company. Users choose whether to request a connection; accepted connections can continue through messaging, multiple meeting-time proposals and secure video access for authenticated participants.

MedicHall is in Open Beta and is seeking practical feedback from medical manufacturers, distributors, suppliers and buyers. It does not claim massive scale, guaranteed business outcomes or automated procurement decisions. The platform is based in İzmir, Türkiye and is available at https://medichall.com.
