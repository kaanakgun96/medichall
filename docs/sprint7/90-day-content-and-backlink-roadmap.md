# 90-day organic content and ethical backlink roadmap

Quality rule: publish one defensible resource rather than several thin AI summaries. Every article needs a named reviewer, source list, original MedicHall workflow example and update date.

## Search landscape observed on 2026-08-13

- Medical Device Distributors ranks with a highly focused European directory, country/category navigation and explicit verification methodology.
- EMDDA and other associations provide authority and member-network context.
- MedicalExpo and other established marketplaces have strong catalog/category authority.
- TenderPulse, TedScout and general procurement-intelligence tools emphasize source breadth, alerts, buyer intelligence and workflow depth.
- MedicHall currently appears for its homepage but has limited distinct indexed search surfaces.

MedicHall’s legitimate gap is the connected, medical-device-specific path from public tender/product/company discovery to private evidence, product terminology, partner fit, RFQ and meeting. Do not copy competitor claims or imply broader source coverage than production provides.

## Days 1–30 — establish the two core topic clusters

### Article 1: How to find medical device distributors in Europe

- Intent: informational → commercial.
- Destination: distributor landing page and company directory.
- Original contribution: role-verification checklist; difference between distributor, manufacturer subsidiary, agent and buyer; how to define the desired product/territory profile.
- Sources: European Commission operator definitions, relevant associations, real public directory examples.

### Article 2: How medical-device manufacturers can monitor European tenders

- Intent: informational → high-intent tender discovery.
- Original contribution: a repeatable workflow using official sources, CPV, product aliases, saved searches and document triage.
- Avoid: claiming MedicHall covers every European notice/source.

### Supporting work

- Reconcile the production blog into version control.
- Add Article/FAQ metadata, author/reviewer and update date to every new article.
- Create a small public methodology page for tender source attribution.

## Days 31–60 — demonstrate expertise with practical references

### Article 3: CPV codes for medical device tenders

- Explain what CPV is and is not.
- Show why keyword/product-taxonomy evidence must complement broad codes.
- Link to official EU procurement references.
- Include a compact, maintained table of genuinely used medical-device code families—do not generate hundreds of doorway pages.

### Article 4: Medical Tender Intelligence explained

- Public notice vs private document analysis.
- Extraction, evidence, unknowns, lots and human review.
- Explain why the official notice remains authoritative.

### Original asset

- Publish a downloadable one-page “Medical Tender Review Checklist” with a stable HTML companion page.
- Require factual/legal review; do not present it as legal advice.

## Days 61–90 — partner discovery and conversion insight

### Article 5: Manufacturer vs distributor — building a European medical-device partnership

- Role, territory, channel, regulatory responsibility, product support and tender capability.
- Link to the mixed directory and structured matchmaking explainer.

### Article 6: How AI can support medical-device partner discovery

- Explicit evidence, missing data, confidence and consent before contact.
- Avoid proprietary scoring details and accuracy claims.

### Evidence report

- Publish an aggregate Open Beta learning note only if real data volume is sufficient and privacy thresholds are met.
- Examples: most common missing profile fields or public tender categories; never expose a company or infer performance from tiny samples.

## Ethical backlink plan

### Tier 1 — direct relevance

- thoughtful editorial pitches to medical-device and healthcare-procurement publications;
- founder interview or contributed article about fragmented procurement/partner discovery;
- association/member profiles where MedicHall is genuinely eligible;
- event/exhibitor profiles at events actually attended;
- public product/company partners linking to their real MedicHall showroom.

### Tier 2 — ecosystem authority

- reputable Turkish and European startup directories with editorial standards;
- accelerator/incubator profile only if genuinely affiliated;
- university/alumni profile only with a real relationship;
- curated B2B SaaS directories where the category and listing are accurate.

### Tier 3 — useful resources

- CPV reference cited by procurement writers;
- public tender-review checklist;
- transparent methodology pages;
- original, aggregate-safe Open Beta observations once sample size permits.

Reject:

- paid link packages, private blog networks and bulk directory submissions;
- automated comment/forum links;
- reciprocal partner-logo walls without real relationships;
- copied guest articles;
- fake awards, reviews or testimonials.

## Programmatic SEO decision

Do not launch country/category combinations in Sprint 7. A useful page would need enough current, unique public tenders or approved companies; crawlable pagination; expiry handling; official attribution; and original context. Re-evaluate when at least 10 substantive current entities exist per proposed page and the page can remain useful when search engines are ignored.

## Measurement

For each resource record:

- indexed canonical URL;
- non-branded impressions and queries;
- organic/referral visitors;
- qualified sign-up and activation events;
- connection/RFQ/meeting actions;
- earned editorial links and referring domains;
- factual update date.

Do not evaluate an article only by page views.
