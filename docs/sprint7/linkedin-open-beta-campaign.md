## Post 2 — The problem

Medical-device business development rarely happens in one place.

A company directory in one tab. Tender portals in another. Long PDFs, product spreadsheets, emails and meeting links spread across the rest.

MedicHall is our attempt to reduce that fragmentation.

Start with a product, company or public tender. Review the evidence. Decide whether the opportunity or partner is relevant. Then move into a direct business workflow.

The official source and the human decision stay in control.

## Post 3 — AI Matchmaking

A business match should explain itself.

MedicHall compares explicit company evidence—products offered and sought, markets, certifications and commercial preferences—then shows supported drivers, gaps and uncertainty.

A score does not contact another company automatically. The user decides whether to request a connection.

That connection can continue through messaging, meeting proposals and secure video.

## Post 4 — Tender Intelligence

European medical tenders can arrive as long, multi-file document packages with lots, technical terms and deadlines hidden across the evidence.

MedicHall separates two jobs:

1. Public discovery: factual tender metadata and the official source.
2. Private intelligence: document extraction, evidence, product context and grounded questions inside an authenticated workspace.

The AI helps navigate the material. It does not replace the source documents or submit a bid.
